04 · Creative

THE OTHER
SIDE

Tech isn't the only language I speak. I've always had a creative brain — one that finds rhythm in systems and systems in rhythm. Here's what that looks like outside of IT.

🎹

Music

Piano, Guitar & Percussion

Taught music at high school level for over 3 years. Coached students through ABRSM examinations across multiple instruments. I don't just play — I teach people how to hear.

🎛️

Production

Music Production

Building tracks from scratch. Arrangement, mixing, sound design. My ear is trained — both musically and technically. Production taught me patience, attention to detail, and creative problem-solving.

🎬

Visual

Video Editing

Post-production, cuts, colour, pacing. If there's a story in the footage, I'll find it. Video editing is just another system to understand and master.

🏀

Physical

Basketball & Gaming

High-focus, fast-decision environments. Basketball taught me how to read a room fast and adapt. Gaming sharpened my pattern recognition and strategic thinking — skills that transfer directly into IT work.

The connection

The same brain that hears a chord progression and understands why it resolves — is the same brain that looks at a system error and traces it back to root cause. Creativity and technical thinking aren't opposites. They're the same muscle, different weights.
